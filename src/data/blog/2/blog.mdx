This is a brain dump.

I've been practising some NeetCode recently and I realised I have forgotten the feeling of pride in writing unassisted code 
how I did at university, when I coded on paper.

Now with IDE autocomplete and AI help it costs next to nothing to iterate.

But I've also become very aware of [sunk-cost-fallacy](https://thedecisionlab.com/biases/the-sunk-cost-fallacy), 
in abandoning progress when I have invested time.

However the positive side of sentimentality is, coding becomes an art. I take more pride and place more effort into making
pretty code.

I have also development of a velocity addiction in the industry, where when test suites pass - that is
where refinement is complete for a change, even for production environments. I think we should bring back some pride and take
more care in what we ship.

One could argue that prettiness does not always deliver business value and it will mostly be agents reading it anyway, which
may be right in the future - but time will tell.
